Our place

At home in Haiku

Forward Maui is in Haiku, on Maui's north shore, where the trade winds meet the reef and windsurfers from everywhere come to sail.

We carry Goya Windsurfing, Quatro, KT Surfing and MFC Hawaii. Boards, sails, foils, wings, fins and the small parts that save a session, all in one place.
