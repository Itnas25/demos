Haiku, Maui

At home on the north shore

Forward Maui is in Haiku, on Maui's north shore, where the trade winds meet the reef and windsurfers from everywhere come to sail.

We carry four brands: Goya Windsurfing, Quatro, KT Surfing and MFC Hawaii. Boards, sails, foils, wings, fins and the small parts that save a session, all in one place. The flagship boards even carry the town's name.
